Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 21, 2026

emergency Our Philosophy

MK Chats is built on the principle of absolute privacy. We believe that your conversations are yours alone. Our architecture is designed to minimize data collection and maximize security through end-to-end encryption.

shield End-to-End Encryption

All messages sent through MK Chats are encrypted on your device and can only be decrypted by the intended recipient. We do not have the keys to read your messages, nor do we store them in a readable format on our servers.

database Information We Collect

  • Account Information: We require a phone number and email for account registration and recovery purposes.
  • Profile Data: Names and profile pictures are shared only with the contacts you interact with.
  • Metadata: We store minimal metadata necessary for message delivery, which is regularly purged.

lock Your Rights

You have the right to delete your account at any time. Upon deletion, all associated data, including your public key and profile information, will be permanently removed from our active systems.
