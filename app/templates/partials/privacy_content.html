01

Our Philosophy

MK Chats is architected on the principle of unyielding privacy. We believe that digital sovereignty is a fundamental right. Our systems are engineered to eliminate unnecessary data points and ensure that your conversations remain encrypted and inaccessible to everyone but you and your recipient.

02

End-to-End Encryption

Every message packet is encrypted on your local client using advanced cryptographic standards before it ever hits our transit network. We never hold your private decryption keys, meaning we cannot read your messages even if compelled.

"Privacy isn't just a policy here; it's the core architectural constraint."
03

Information We (Don't) Collect

  • Minimal Account Metadata

    We require only an email and phone number to establish your secure node identity and facilitate recovery.

  • Ephemeral Traffic Data

    Routing metadata is processed in RAM and purged immediately after successful message delivery.

04

Data Retention

Inactive account data is completely eradicated from our clusters after 30 days of deletion request. We do not participate in commercial data harvesting or long-term archiving of user interactions.

05

Third-party Protocols

We utilize isolated, hardened cloud environments for message relay. These providers see only encrypted blobs and have no visibility into the content or participants of any exchange.

06

Cookies & Local Storage

We use session-specific local storage only to maintain your cryptographic state. We do not use third-party analytics or advertising trackers.
